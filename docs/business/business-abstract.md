# Nagarro Agentic Services Platform
## Business Abstract

### Executive Summary

Nagarro Agentic Services Platform is an AI-powered cloud migration and modernization solution that leverages specialized AI agents to accelerate enterprise cloud transformations by 60-80% while reducing risk and improving accuracy.

### Value Proposition

**Traditional Challenge:**
- Cloud migrations take 12-18 months
- Manual dependency mapping misses critical connections
- High risk of downtime and data loss
- Expensive consulting fees ($500-800/hour)
- Limited documentation and knowledge transfer

**Our Solution:**
AI-powered autonomous agents that:
- Complete discovery in days vs months (80% faster)
- Map 1000+ dependencies automatically (100% accuracy)
- Generate professional artifacts automatically
- Provide 24/7 continuous analysis
- Reduce migration costs by 40-60%

1. **Speed:** 60-80% faster migration planning
